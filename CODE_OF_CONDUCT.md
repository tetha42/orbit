# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive and specific when reviewing code.
- Assume good intent, but challenge unsafe technical decisions.
- Respect privacy: never publish another member's personal data, credentials or
  private messages.
- Give credit for work and document decisions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, publishing private data,
or deliberately introducing security backdoors are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation
when this policy is violated. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainers through the repository's private contact channel.
